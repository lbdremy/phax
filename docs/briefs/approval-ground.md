Write the phax spec `approval-ground`: a plan is never stale because of its own approval. Fixing it closes the last of the two happy-path defects that block 1.0 (NEXT_STEPS.md §"Road to 1.0.0", "Two known happy-path defects"; the other, preflight-before-naming, landed 2026-10-03).

The defect, as recorded in NEXT_STEPS.md (§"Small follow-ups", "A plan whose footprint names `docs/plans/approvals.json` is stale at its own approval", found 2026-09-10 launching the artifact-timestamp-naming plan):
- `phax artifact approve` records the baseline at HEAD, then commits the approval: a rewrite of `docs/plans/approvals.json` and of the plan's own frontmatter (`approved: { date, baseline }`). `phax run` then computes staleness (`src/app/planStaleness.ts`, `computeStaleness` in `src/domain/artifact/lineage.ts`): the files changed since the baseline that are in the plan's footprint are `ground-changed` evidence. A plan whose footprint names `docs/plans/approvals.json` — optional files count too — is therefore stale before it starts. The workaround so far: leave both ledgers out of such a plan's file lists, which a plan that really works on approvals cannot do.
- The fix direction noted then: exclude the transition commit from the ground-change window (the baseline becomes the approval commit, or the transition's own write-set is ignored), covered by a staleness test.

What the spec must cover:
- Exactly which changes stop counting as ground change: only the plan's own approval commit, or every lifecycle transition commit (`phax artifact approve|abandon|complete|…`, which write the ledgers, frontmatter and `archive/` moves) made after the baseline. Say what each choice abandons: a later approval of another plan also rewrites `approvals.json` and, under the narrow choice, still makes this plan stale; under the broad choice, a plan that genuinely depends on the ledger's content no longer sees another transition's edit.
- How phax recognises a transition commit deterministically (its subject `chore(plans|specs): approve|… <slug>`, a trailer, the recorded baseline + the transition's known write-set, or the approval record itself) — no heuristic on content. Prefer what is already persisted; say if a commit trailer is added and why.
- `self-changed` and `spec-changed` are unchanged: a plan edited after approval, or whose spec changed, is still stale.
- Where the change applies: `phax run`'s staleness gate, `phax plans status`, `phax resume` if it checks, and `phax plans lint` if it reports staleness — one rule for all.
- The approval record format (`docs/plans/approvals.json`) does not change shape; say whether `baseline` keeps its meaning (HEAD before the approval commit) or moves to the approval commit, and what that does to records written before the fix (they must keep working, with no migration).
- Acceptance criteria: a plan whose footprint names `docs/plans/approvals.json` (and one naming its own path) is fresh right after `approve`; it becomes stale when a real change lands on a footprint file after approval; the existing staleness reasons keep their behaviour.
- Out of scope, named: the conflict between a run's completion commit and approvals made on main during the run (NEXT_STEPS "A run's completion conflicts with approvals made on main during the run" — a merge question, its own spec); any change to the approval or completion transitions themselves.
